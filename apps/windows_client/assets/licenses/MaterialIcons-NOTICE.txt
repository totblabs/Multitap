Material Icons by Google
========================

The application includes a subset of the Material Icons font distributed with
Flutter. The Flutter build removes unused glyphs from the font.

The license accompanying this pinned Flutter font artifact is Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International. Its complete text is provided in
MaterialIcons-CC-BY-4.0.txt.
